# Setting Ground-Truth — Season of Ghosts (Willowshore, Shenmen / Tian Xia)

Hook: `canon`. This file is GROUND-TRUTH: it overrides recollection. Consult before
inventing places, NPCs, faiths, dates, or faction stances. When something isn't here,
generate via `generators/` and write it back. Spoiler-grade campaign-role facts are tagged
**[GM-ONLY]** — never volunteer them to the player; reveal only through play.

---

## TONE

Folk horror + cozy community + mystery. Gothic, not survival horror — dread comes from
**mood**, the safety of named neighbors, and slow revelation, NOT from punishing combat.
A small rural town the players come to love, threatened by curses, ghosts, and a greedy
outside world. Mysteries reward investigation, downtime, and relationships. Wood (the town,
the living, the humble) vs. metal (the opposition) is a quiet running motif.

## PLACES

### Shenmen & the wider region
- **Shenmen** — haunted nation in central Tian Xia, inspired by horror traditions (not a real
  culture). History: conquered by Lung Wa, took Hwanggot immigrants, later seized by jorogumo.
  Ghosts and monsters are a real part of life; practices arise from necessity, not superstition.
- **Specterwood** — the vast temperate forest enclosing Willowshore's hinterlands. Notorious
  for haunts/ghosts elsewhere, but the immediate hinterlands have been (traditionally) safe.
- **Sze** — closest large city; ruled by Chou Mingxia, who died and returned as a ghost (7107).
  Source of the lumber demand that shaped Willowshore.
- **Karahai** — coastal fortified market east of town, Willowshore's nearest neighbor (a day's
  ride) and main economic link. **[GM-ONLY]** Mago Kai's base of operations in 7223.
- **Sea of Ghosts** — the eastern sea the Duyue eventually reaches. Tianjing lies on its far shore.
- **Off-map roads:** Central Northern (to Moon Marsh, ruined village); Northeastern (cattle
  ranch of a ruthless Sze expatriate); Northwest Trail (overgrown, to an abandoned shrine);
  Eastern Northern (to Foxhollow, 3 days, then the Gossamer Mountains); Eastern (to Karahai);
  Southern / "Apple Road" (wild apple grove, populous towns days south).

### Willowshore — rivers & banks
- **Ceiba River** — flows east through the center of town into the larger **Duyue River**;
  20–25 ft deep; only skiffs/rowboats west of the dam. Town founded on this tributary.
- **Ceiba Creek** — shallow tributary, rarely >6 ft deep.
- **Duyue River** — largest waterway in the region.
- **Southbank** (south of the Ceiba) — original/founder families, Southbank faction heartland.
- **Northridge** (north bank, on a prominent incline) — post-Lung-Wa arrivals; Northridge faction.

## FACTIONS

Allegiance is NOT strictly by residence; many residents are **Unaffiliated** (default
attitude indifferent). Both factions agree on one thing: Willowshore's survival comes first.

- **Southbank (traditionalists)** — descend from the original monastery-era settlers (south
  bank). Value tradition, self-sufficiency, farming, oral history, building by hand. **On
  principle hold jorogumo to be evil monsters, best avoided.** Spokesman: **Old Matsuki**.
- **Northridge (pragmatists)** — post-Lung-Wa arrivals (lumber workers, merchants, ex-imperial
  guards; north bank, on the incline). Value modern pragmatism, urban growth, trade, learning
  (a school/library/university dream). **Many are open to jorogumo ruling Shenmen.**
- **The jorogumo question** — the divide over whether jorogumo rule is acceptable is the live
  political wedge. As the AP opens it is still "ghost story / scary rumor" — no jorogumo has
  visited Willowshore yet (as far as anyone knows). Tengu PCs: see HARD CONTENT LINES.

### Reputation
PCs build **Reputation Points** with each faction (start 0/0). Tiers set each faction NPC's
default attitude, a social-DC modifier, and Earn-Income bonuses:

| Reputation | Points | Default Attitude | Social DC | Earn Income |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revered | 31+ | Helpful | –10 | +3 (treat as 2 lvls higher) |
| Admired | 15–30 | Friendly | –5 | +2 (1 lvl higher) |
| Liked | 5–14 | Friendly | –2 | +1 |
| Ignored | –4 to 4 | Indifferent | 0 | — |
| Disliked | –5 to –14 | Unfriendly | +2 | –1 |
| Hated | –15 to –29 | Unfriendly | +5 | –3 |
| Hunted | –30 or less | Hostile | +10 | can't Earn Income |

Locations carry a **Southbank** or **Northridge** trait (some Unaffiliated). Making the NPC who
runs a site Helpful earns its **support** (and +1 Rep with that faction the first time, unless
already admired/revered). Locations grant Earn-Income, Crafting (full price, 4-day delay), etc.
**Teeth-Clenching Teamwork** (above) lets PCs lean on neighbors across faction lines.

## FAITHS & SHRINES

Two **temples**: Daikitsu (W9 Nine Ear Shrine) and Pharasma (W10 Lady of Souls). Many also
worship **Abadar** and **Lady Nanbyo** (the "Widow of Suffering"; her drowned shrine is now
Woodraft Lake, W13). Townsfolk are religiously diverse; small shrines dot the town.

- **Shrines present** (a worshipper automatically knows the location): Abadar, Calistria,
  Daikitsu, Desna, Ketephys, Kofusachi, Lao Shu Po, Nalinivati, Pharasma, Qi Zhong, Shelyn,
  Shizuru, Tsukiyo, Yaezhing, **and** Lady Nanbyo. (Desna's shrine is abandoned, in W26.)
- **Sangpotshi** — the **philosophy** the town's founders followed; the Tan Sugi Monastery was
  built to pursue enlightenment through it. Many in Willowshore still walk this path.
- **Timeline note:** Arazni, Casandalee, Nocticula faiths don't yet exist / haven't changed;
  Aroden recently dead but never significant in Tian Xia. Avoid these for worship in 7108.

## TIMELINE / HISTORY  (Imperial Calendar; AR = IC − 2500)

- **6223 IC [GM-ONLY-leaning]** — hero **Tan Sui-Jing** defeats the fiend **Kugaptee** but dies
  as the battle ends; her body sprouts a **sugi tree** over Kugaptee's remains, trapping him
  below the roots. (Known in town as a folk legend / town legacy; the fiend's name is GM-ONLY.)
- **7020** — **Zhi Hui**, a Sangpotshi master/monk, finds the sugi tree and founds the **Tan
  Sugi Monastery** around it in the Trimountain hinterlands. **Willowshore** is founded to
  support the builders; supply merchants nickname it "the shore of willows" (= "impossible to
  find"). The **Pilgrim's Path** connects them. Zhi Hui gifts the **Eternal Lantern**.
- **7054** — Zhi Hui dies of natural causes; the monastery declines; town's boom fades.
- **7060** — Willowshore becomes a **lumber town** for Lung Wa (bridges, dam built). Monks
  drift off; monastery abandoned. Local Sangpotshi tradition dwindles.
- **7062** — Woodcutters try to harvest the sacred sugi grove. Disaster: they return as undead
  with broken weapons; dozens of townsfolk die overnight. The **Night of Broken Blades.** The
  monastery is thereafter shunned as cursed/haunted. **[GM-ONLY]** this was Chou Mingxia's son's
  attempt; it let Kugaptee's influence begin to spread.
- **7102** — Governor **Heh Shan-Bao** installed by Lung Wa.
- **7106** — **Age of Lost Omens begins; Lung Wa collapses.** Worst winter storms in memory.
  Shenmen's officials, cut off, turn cruel. **Lumber lords abandon their estates and flee**
  (the "lumber lords exodus") — a relief to locals, who let the buildings be reclaimed by woods.
  Most soldiers leave; a few who put down roots stay.
- **7107** — Jorogumo **Lang Loi** seizes control of Shenmen **[GM-ONLY name]**. Chou Mingxia of
  Sze is slain and **returns as a ghost.** (Both reach Willowshore only as rumor.)
- **7108** — **THE PRESENT** (campaign opens on the first day of summer). Two years post-collapse;
  town nervous about winter. Jorogumo rule is still "scary rumor." **[GM-ONLY]** Late spring,
  Heh Shan-Bao, learning of the jorogumo takeover, attempts a ritual to heal the Tan Sugi and
  seal Kugaptee. It backfires and kills the entire town in their sleep; their souls are trapped,
  unaware, in a **cyclic mindscape** that resets every last-day-of-spring. Heh becomes trapped
  alone in a second nested mindscape ("a haunting within a haunting").
- **[GM-ONLY] 7223** — true present in the living world; 115 years of resets later. Lumber baron
  **Mago Kai** (sent by ghost-Chou Mingxia of Sze) reaches ruined Willowshore, bases at Karahai,
  and begins a mass exorcism/cleansing — disrupting the loop enough that the PCs can finally
  break free. The PCs and townsfolk have unknowingly relived 7108 for the 115th time.

## THE EIGHT PRACTICES

Local advice followed during the first weeks of summer (the season of ghosts). Whether they
actually ward off spirits is hotly debated in town.
1. Do not call a ghost a ghost.
2. Do not pat people on the head or shoulders.
3. Avoid entering bodies of water when a ghost is nearby.
4. Do not eat food that has two stick-like objects protruding from it.
5. Do not lean against walls during the day.
6. Do not whistle at night.
7. Do not leave laundry out at night.
8. If you hear someone call your name from behind you at night, do not turn around.

(The **Reenactment Festival**, last day of spring, stages fake abductions by townsfolk in
paper masks to trick real ghosts into thinking the town is already haunted — the PCs are this
year's "abductees," which is how the campaign opens.)

## HARD CONTENT LINES

- **Horror & Consent** — this is a horror campaign. Establish a session-zero understanding of
  comfort. If the player is not comfortable with a horror trope/theme, downplay or remove it.
  Honor lines and veils; check in. (Paizo "Horror and Consent.")
- **Arachnophobia** — spiders/jorogumo imagery recur as both allies and foes. If the player is
  uncomfortable, reskin spider creatures to another unsettling animal (toad, snake, vulture,
  worm) and keep art hidden, preserving dread without the phobia trigger.
- **Tengu animosity (safety note)** — jorogumo (Shenmen's rulers) loathe tengu and fly into
  murderous rages around them. At the AP's start no jorogumo has reached remote Willowshore, so
  a tengu PC rarely faces this. Willowshore itself harbors **no** intrinsic anti-tengu bias
  (Kawaka is respected). **If** rumor/news of a jorogumo visit arises, check in with the player
  before deploying this animosity; handle it in a way that's comfortable for the table — never
  spring it as a "gotcha."
