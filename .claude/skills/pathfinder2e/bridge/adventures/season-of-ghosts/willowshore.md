## Diminisher (solo scaling)
This slice is the persistent setting gazetteer — it contains no set-piece combats of its own (the town encounters live in the act slices). Hinterland random/wandering encounters here assume 2nd-level PCs and do NOT scale. For solo play, default Diminisher 1/2 on any wandering-monster combat drawn against a single PC; from Act 2 onward, prefer narrating hinterland encounters rather than running them (per the book's pacing guidance).
