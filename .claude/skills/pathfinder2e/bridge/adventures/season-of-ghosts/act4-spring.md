# Ingested Adventure — Season of Ghosts, Act 4 "To Bloom Below the Web" (act4-spring)   (hook: adventure-ingest; pure sandbox)
fidelity: light

Scope: the final act. Willowshore has been returned to the LIVING world (the ritual at the end of Act 3 succeeded). Spring is mild and sunny. Covers: waking near the Eternal Lantern and reckoning with a century lost; the ruined governor's manor (Ch.11) and the False Governor nindoru ambush; reopening trade — rebuilding Karahai Bridge and the Silkwasp Bandits (Lo Kuon) at the bridge and at their camp (jiang-shi Hong Meigui); Shinzo's farewell; the jorogumo princess Ren Mei Li's return, her feline emissary Amai, and the Five-Season Treasures stewardship trial (Ch.12); the parallel murder-mystery (Heh Shan-Bao the yohoi kills Oono, Elizeth Candora, You So-Jin); side foes (Isori the naga, khravgodons, algrievers, path maidens, the Un-Weavers quintessivores); the Fang and Key; and the climax (Ch.13): the Restored Manor mindscape, Kugaptee's Grasp, Kugaptee's Grave (four memory battles), Below Kugaptee's Claw, freeing/fighting Heh Shan-Bao, Kugaptee's Final Death, and Tan Sui-Jing's passing of the burden. Source pages p.215-272. PCs are 10th-11th level, reaching ~12th-13th by the finale. (Pages 273+ — Continuing the Campaign, First Long Night, Sangpotshi — are appendix/continuation material out of this slice's scope.)

SPOILER NOTE: This act is built on revelations the GM must WITHHOLD until earned. The biggest: the friendly "returned" Governor Heh Shan-Bao in the ruined manor is a nindoru impostor (the False Governor), and the REAL Heh Shan-Bao is now a yohoi/monster who is murdering townsfolk and rules a corrupted mindscape (Kugaptee's Grasp) as Kugaptee's nascent vessel. The murders are HIS; the Five-Season Treasures trial is partly Ren Mei Li's loyalty test, but its real revelation (via the path maiden's mirror) is the killer's identity and the Fang-and-Key path to him. Ren Mei Li's offer secretly seeks fealty to HERSELF over the jorogumo court. The finale lets the PCs FREE Heh Shan-Bao (Influence) rather than only kill him, and reveals that Tan Sui-Jing — the ancient heroine reincarnated as the sugi tree — restores the PCs and the tree. Fragments carrying these are flagged spoiler:true.

Default solo scaling for this act's combats is 1/2 (halve enemy counts / use the weaker side of scaling), as with prior acts. Many encounters here are already calibrated as group set-pieces at the high end (Severe/Extreme 10-12), so a solo PC should lean hard on the diminisher and on the act's many non-combat outs: paying/parleying the Silkwasps, talking down Isori/Gurglegut/Ithiniak/the algrievers, the Influence route to FREE Heh Shan-Bao, and freeing the chained souls to weaken the finale (each freed soul strips a defense from Heh and reduces shisagishin count). Notable scaling notes: the Silkwasp Camp and the Below-the-Claw fight already scale by circumstance (surviving bridge bandits; number of souls freed) — apply the diminisher ON TOP of choosing the lower-population variant. The four-round Kugaptee's Final Death cataclysm is environmental (basic saves) and is best neutralized by carrying all four life-links rather than scaled.
