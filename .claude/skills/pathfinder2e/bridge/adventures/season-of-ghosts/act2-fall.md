# Ingested Adventure — Season of Ghosts, Act 2 "Let the Leaves Fall" (act2-fall)   (hook: adventure-ingest; pure sandbox)
fidelity: light

Scope: defending Willowshore through fall. Preparation Points & weekly downtime; the 12-week fall timeline of events and hauntings; restoring the Cerulean Teahouse; researching the curse; First Long Night; the Feast of the Kami; and the journey beyond the Wall of Ghosts along the Pilgrim's Path to the Tan Sugi monastery and Kugaptee's grave. Source pages p.93-149. PCs begin this act 4th level, should reach 7th by Act 3.

SPOILER NOTE: Several beats record campaign-central secrets the GM must WITHHOLD until earned in play — the true nature of the hauntings (they are living exorcists led by lumber baron Mago Kai in the modern world, NOT the curse), the truth that Willowshore is a cyclic mindscape (not a curse), that the missing governor Heh Shan-Bao will never return as the locals hope, that Yami is a bakeneko, and the deepest truth (revealed in Act 3) that the townsfolk are dead. The Kugaptee storyline is its own secret: Kugaptee is a dead nindoru demigod whose grave under the Tan Sugi tree slowly corrupts the mindscape; Xin Yue works to revive him. Fragments carrying these are flagged spoiler:true.

## Diminisher (solo scaling): 1/2
Most combats are built for four PCs (the AP states so). For solo or small-party play, apply the standard mythic-gm Diminisher of 1/2 to combat encounters: roughly halve enemy counts (e.g. 2 amphisbaenas -> 1; 4 caretakers -> 2; 6 monks of Kugaptee -> 3) and consider dropping unique bosses by one elite step or trimming their HP. Haunts and hazards scale poorly by count — keep haunt mechanics intact but lower save/Disable DCs by ~2 if the solo PC is under-leveled. The E16 climax is "well beyond Extreme 6" even for four PCs and explicitly relies on Zhi Hui's intervention; for solo play lean hard on that intervention (have the roots remove most kurobozus and let Zhi Hui heal) and run only Xin Yue + a reduced second wave.
