# Ingested Adventure — Season of Ghosts: BESTIARY & KEY NPCS   (hook: adventure-ingest; pure sandbox)
fidelity: light

This slice is a STAT TOOLBOX, not a scene list. These clusters let the GM run foes/NPCs straight from the book rather than improvising from level benchmarks. Surface a creature fragment when that foe enters play; surface the named-NPC fragments when those characters appear. SPOILER fragments record hidden campaign truth (Heh Shan-Bao's fate, Xin Yue's role, Kugaptee, the noppera-bo impersonators) — withhold from the player until earned in fiction. Diminisher applies to combats: for solo play scale encounters down (default 1/2) and prefer single foes; numbers below are the book's full-party stat blocks.

### cluster: best.nindoru — "Nindoru Fiends (the campaign's signature foes)"   (source: p.329)
scene: Nindorus are UNHOLY fiends born when an evil, oft-reincarnated soul is wrenched from the cycle of souls and corrupted. Living (not undead) despite decayed/broken looks; wield broken-yet-functional weapons; manifest objects/creatures from thought (the blood-red butterflies are physical thoughts). Shared traits: darkvision, immune death effects, weakness silver 5 (except argyrzei), resist void 5, and Soul Shred (auto-counteract any attempt to resurrect/animate a creature they killed — counteract rank scales with type). They savor souls that have reincarnated many times — ALL Season of Ghosts PCs qualify (DC 11 flat check if ever in doubt). Associated with the Universe, like rakshasas. Kugaptee, the buried demigod under the Tan Sugi, is the campaign's apex nindoru "ascetic."

### cluster: best.noppera-bo — "Noppera-bo (faceless yokai)"   (source: p.339)
scene: Faceless yokai who delight in deceit; look human from behind, even like someone you know, until they turn. Shared No Face trait: no vision but PRECISE hearing; don't breathe (immune inhaled/olfactory/visual); feed by absorbing fluid through skin; weakness SENSITIVE HEARING (deafened → slowed 1). They steal/wear faces. Bitter enemies of dalgyal gwishin. The deadliest variant in this campaign — the IMPERSONATOR — is a manifestation of Kugaptee's power inside Governor Heh Shan-Bao's mindscape, copies of the PCs themselves.
