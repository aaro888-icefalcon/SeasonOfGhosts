# Ingested Adventure — Season of Ghosts, Act 3 "No Breath to Cry" (act3-winter)   (hook: adventure-ingest; pure sandbox)
fidelity: light

Scope: Willowshore through a cruel winter (Chapters 8–9 and the transmigration climax). Source pages p.151-193. PCs begin this act ~7th level and should reach ~8th-9th by its end. The act's engine is Preparation Points (Hope/Food/Security) drained by weekly attrition and winter events, against a 13-event winter timeline. Centerpieces: the red-smoke exorcism rescue at Cloud Paper House; researching and casting mindscape shift to reach Governor Heh Shan-Bao's parallel mindscape; the chase, manor wards, séance-like confession ("Know and Despair") and the noppera-bo impersonators; Shinzo's full reveal of what the PCs are; researching/casting transmigrate to return (as spirits) to the living world; and a gauntlet of winter events (the fox ghost Rin, the silsyche Tenon, the crane-wife, the Claylight Festival, the nosois' tea, the séance with Mago Kai's exorcists, and the jorogumo princess Ren Mei Li). It ends in the Between-Life-and-Death borderland against three kagekuma nindorus, which delivers the PCs (as ectoplasmic spirits) to Act 4.

SPOILER NOTE: This act delivers the campaign's CENTRAL REVELATION — the townsfolk of Willowshore are DEAD, killed when Governor Heh Shan-Bao's ritual failed, and they live trapped in a repeating year-long mindscape; the "hauntings" are modern-world exorcists (lumber baron Mago Kai's crew) 115 years in the future trying to banish the ruins. Heh Shan-Bao was devoured body-mind-soul by the dead nindoru demigod Kugaptee; his mindscape is the dying flicker of his consciousness. Shinzo is a shinigami (god of death) under punishment from Pharasma. This is the PCs' LAST cycle (115th). The Eternal Lantern's flame has been stolen to Karahai, limiting transmigrate to a temporary 4th-rank casting. Fragments carrying these are flagged spoiler:true — the GM must record them but WITHHOLD them until earned (most are gated to be revealed only at/after the Heh Shan-Bao confrontation and Shinzo's answers).
