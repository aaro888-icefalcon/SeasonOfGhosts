# Ingested Adventure — Season of Ghosts: Adventure Toolbox   (hook: adventure-ingest; pure sandbox)
fidelity: light
slice: toolbox   source: hardcover.md p.286-323   act: toolbox (rewards & rules, all acts)

This slice is the AP's **toolbox**: the Sangpotshi philosophy and cycle-of-life rules, the GM-facing nindoru lore (a held secret), and the rewards/tools the world can surface — magic tea, Willowshore fulus, magic items, spells, rituals, spirit powers, and reincarnation feats. Most clusters are **catalogs of ELEMENT fragments**: each item/power/spell/feat is a discrete reward the GM can drop as loot, shop stock, or earned boon. Treat levels/prices/DCs/activations as load-bearing. The two **secret** clusters (Sangpotshi/Nindoru lore) carry the campaign's central twist and must be withheld until earned.

---

## Diminisher (solo scaling)
**1/2.** This slice is mostly non-combat (rewards/rules), so few direct encounters arise here. Where toolbox content drives a fight — silversoul bombs/anti-nindoru spells deployed against a manifested nindoru, the two ghost commoners on a crit-failed *open the wall of ghosts*, or the border-between-life-and-death encounter triggered by *transmigrate* (p.191) — apply the default solo Diminisher of 1/2 to enemy count/HP, per the engine's PF2e scaling. Spirit powers themselves substantially buff a transmigrated solo PC, so lean on the standard 1/2 rather than scaling further up.
