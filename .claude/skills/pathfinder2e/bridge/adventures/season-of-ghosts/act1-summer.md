# Ingested Adventure — Season of Ghosts, Act 1: "The Summer That Never Was"   (hook: adventure-ingest; pure sandbox)
fidelity: light
source: hardcover.md p.26-88 (Chapters 1-4)
slice: act1-summer

> SANDBOX NOTE: Clusters surface by contextual relevance, never in forced order. No climax armor.
> The PCs begin at 1st level and should reach 4th by act's end (2nd after the second downtown ringleader; 3rd before Chapter 4).
>
> CENTRAL SECRET — GM ONLY, WITHHOLD: Willowshore is a **looping mindscape**. 115 years ago Governor Heh Shan-Bao's
> protective ritual against the fiend **Kugaptee** failed, killing everyone in town and trapping their souls. Each year the
> cycle resets to the first day of summer with no memories. This year the stone-spider guardian "Ugly Cute" died in the real
> world, weakening protection: Kugaptee began to wake, spawned monsters from his thoughts, raised a "Wall of Ghosts" around
> his grave, and — for the first time — the monster-mayhem did NOT reset. The PCs (and everyone in town) are dead souls in a
> dream-loop. They do not know this. Mark every fragment that reveals or hints at it spoiler:true and feed it out only as
> earned. Even Ugly Cute thinks the 115 years were "a dream." Shinzo (a shinigami) and his raven Yix (a psychopomp) secretly
> work to free the trapped souls. Reveal these truths gradually across later acts.

## Diminisher (solo scaling)
Default **1/2** for combats in this slice (a 4-PC AP run by one or two solo PCs). Halve enemy counts and round down, keeping at least the named/unique foe.
- Trivial/Low fights (centipedes, jinkin packs, swarms, hunting spiders, grunts): halve the count.
- Unique bosses (Gurglegut, Gray Butcher, Mo Douqiu, Zoudou, Mugirou, Great Willow, Xungu, Ugly Cute's living boulders, Fan Hongrui): keep the unique; halve accompanying minions/occultists/grunts.
- Extreme/Severe "not-meant-to-fight" set-pieces (the five Happy Kappas, four-jinkin chase at Dawnstep, the Abacus Sisters) should stay social/avoidance encounters — don't scale them into winnable brawls; they signal "flee or talk."
- Hazards (Grasping Currents, Wood Pile, Summoning Runes) keep their DCs; they don't scale by party size.
- For a lone PC, drop to **1/3** on Moderate+ encounters and lean on allied NPCs (Hai-er, Sumika, town guards, Ugly Cute, freed kappa) as combat support per the text.
