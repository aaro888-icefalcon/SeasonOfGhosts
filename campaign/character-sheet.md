## Class features
- **Devise a Stratagem** — start of turn (free) or 1 action: pick a creature, pre-roll a d20; your Strikes vs it this round use that roll, **and you may use Int (+4) to hit** with a finesse/agile/unarmed/ranged weapon.
- **Strategic Strike** — +**1d6** precision damage on a Devised hit.
- **On the Case / Pursue a Lead** — keep up to 2 leads; +1 circumstance to checks to investigate a lead.
- **Methodology: Alchemical Sciences** — **Alchemical Crafting** + trained Crafting + formula book; while pursuing a lead, **1 action to create one alchemical item** (≤ your level), up to **4×/day** (Int mod).
