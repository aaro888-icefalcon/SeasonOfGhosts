# The Party — GM-run companions (Daegon's fellow festival abductees)

Three Willowshore locals, woven from real friends. GM voices & rolls them; player may direct.
All level 1. Diminisher = 1 (full party of 4 with Daegon → encounters run at full published strength).

---

## Su-Won — Half-elf (aiuvarin) **Monk 1**   *(patterned on Alex: laconic, fearless)*
Quiet man who learned the surviving Sangpotshi forms near the ruined monastery. Says ten words a day and walks straight at what scares everyone else.
- **HP 20** · **AC 17** · Speed **30 ft** (Nimble Elf) · low-light vision · Perception **+6**
- **Str 12(+1) Dex 18(+4) Con 14(+2) Int 10 Wis 16(+3) Cha 10**
- **Saves:** Fort **+7**, Ref **+9**, Will **+8** (expert all three — hard to rattle)
- **Fists** +7, 1d6+1 B, agile/finesse/nonlethal · **Flurry of Blows** (1 action = two fist Strikes; MAP on the 2nd)
- **Tiger Stance** (1 action to enter): tiger claws +7, **1d8+1 S** agile/finesse; while in stance, Step moves 10 ft
- Skills: Acrobatics +7, Athletics +4, Stealth +7, Religion +6

## Lanhua "Lan" — Gnome **Druid 1** (Leaf order)   *(patterned on Denice: kind, quiet, iconoclastic)*
A small, gentle gardener who'd rather keep company with growing things than the town's metal-and-money bustle. Fey-touched; a little leshy familiar (**Pak**, a vine-curl the size of a cat) rides her shoulder.
- **HP 18** · **AC 16** · Small · Speed 25 ft · low-light vision · Perception **+7**
- **Str 8(−1) Dex 14(+2) Con 14(+2) Int 12(+1) Wis 18(+4) Cha 12(+1)**
- **Saves:** Fort +5, Ref +5, **Will +9**
- **Primal prepared caster** — Spell DC **17**, spell attack **+7**
  - Cantrips: *electric arc* (2 targets, 1d4+4, basic Reflex DC 17), *produce flame*, *shield*, *guidance*, *stabilize*
  - 1st-level slots (2): **heal** (1d8) ×1, **tangle vine** ×1 *(re-preparable each morning)*
  - Focus (1 pt): **Goodberry** (Leaf order — grow berries that heal a little)
- Wild Empathy (Diplomacy with animals) · **Fey Fellowship** (+2 Perception & Diplomacy with fey/kami)
- Skills: Nature +7, Medicine +7, Survival +7, Religion +7, Athletics +2
- Anathema: wanton harm to the natural world

## Tobi — Tengu **Commander 1**   *(patterned on Henry: gregarious)*
A born organizer with a loud laugh and a battered banner on his back; ran festival logistics and bossed the woodcutters' card games. Leads by lifting everyone around him.
- **HP 16** · **AC 18** (scale mail) · Speed 25 ft · low-light vision · Perception **+3**
- **Str 12(+1) Dex 14(+2) Con 14(+2) Int 18(+4) Wis 10 Cha 12(+1)**
- **Saves:** Fort **+7**, Ref +5, **Will +5**
- **Rapier** +5, 1d6+1 P, finesse, deadly d8 · beak +6 (tengu) 1d4+1 P finesse
- **Commander DC 17** · Warfare Lore +7
- **Commander's Banner** (within **30 ft**): allies gain **+1 status bonus to saves vs fear & emotion** *(genre-perfect against the Crimson Moon later)*
- **Tactics** (Deploy a Tactic = 1 action, auditory, allies within 30 ft):
  - **"Strike Hard!"** — one commanded ally may immediately Strike (reaction)
  - **"Form Up!"** — up to two commanded allies may Step or Stride toward an enemy (set flanks)
  - **Combat Assessment** (1 action): Strike + on a hit Recall Knowledge on the target (learn AC/weakness)
- Skills: Society +7, Diplomacy +4, Intimidation +4, Athletics +4, Nature +3, Medicine +3

## Ha Hai-er — Human **town guard** (NPC ally, joined Scene 3)   *Creature 1*
Watchtower survivor; the party unmasked & killed the noppera-bo that wore her comrade Sajan's face and nearly fooled her. Hard, capable, badly shaken; now fiercely loyal to the four who didn't let it see they knew.
- **HP ~20** · **AC 16** · Perception **+6**
- **Crossbow** +7 ranged (1d8 P, reload) · **shortsword** +6 melee (1d6+2 S)
- Fort +8, Ref +6, Will +5
- Knows the town & the Eight Practices cold. **Flees / breaks if reduced under ~6 HP** (she has a family to get back to).
- *Wants:* reach her **daughter** (across the river, with Granny Hu's Northridge folk) and her **husband Edha** (at the Matsuki estate, Southbank). Reuniting her = +1 Reputation with both factions.
